# Public UX benchmark and design decisions

Reviewed 19 November 2026. Evidence comes from public product material. Speed, native device behavior and restricted commercial flows were not tested. The tradeoffs below are inferences from the public workflows.

| Product | Observed workflow and hierarchy | Inferred tradeoff | AutoParts design decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| PartsTech | Search across preferred parts suppliers. The supplier network brings ordering options into one workflow. [Supplier network](https://partstech.com/suppliers-network/) | Local supplier coverage depends on active integrations. Multiple offers add decision density. | Group parts, vehicles, suppliers and locations in one lookup. Show only company stock and permitted supplier records. |
| Epicor Automotive Catalog | Part information, specifications, images, fitment and interchange are distinct catalog capabilities. [Official service description](https://www.epicor.com/contentassets/6b5d4ab0b8d54bbc95b7857c4efb2d0d/epicor%20mca%20automotive%20information%20services%20supplement.pdf) | Comprehensive evidence is useful during inspection. It can be dense for a routine checkout. | Put exact references and scoped stock first. Keep fitment evidence and supporting media distinguishable. |
| Odoo Inventory and POS | Barcode-led receipts follow document, scan, adjust quantity and validate. Warehouse locations and packaging have separate meanings. [Barcode operations](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/operations/receipts_deliveries.html) | Extensive operation and configuration choices add training overhead. | Choose the product and warehouse first. Keep stock validation, PCS/carton quantities and reconciliation separate from catalog edits. |
| Shopify POS | Staff permissions and location-specific tiles allow quick product and cart actions. [Smart grid](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/customize-pos/smart-grid-management) · [POS permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions/pos-permissions) | More shortcuts can crowd the tablet. The retail workflow alone does not establish automotive compatibility. | Use three immediate destinations and two role-aware owner actions. Retain the live cart through inspections and navigation. |

The supplied screenshot informs the home hierarchy: search, task destinations, then priority actions. Workspace tokens cover surfaces, spacing, typography and touch sizing. Existing fields, forms, tabs, tables, status badges and dialogs are reused throughout. Light is the tested default. Dark theme is an open accessibility task across the inherited screens.
