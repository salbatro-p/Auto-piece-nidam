## Money, reservations and shipping (correctness first)
- One authoritative **inventory service** across internal counter sales and ecommerce. Customer baskets do NOT automatically reduce stock unless a deliberate reservation policy is defined. Checkout creates an atomic, finite reservation with TTL and unique idempotency key; payment success -> committed order; failures/timeout/cancellations -> correct release; fulfillment -> deduct from on-hand once and adjust reservation. No double spending stock if order callback, click or webhook is repeated.
- Provider abstraction for payment: start with cash-on-delivery where accepted and sandbox electronic payment. Confirm current official CMI/approved PSP contract, capabilities and 3D Secure workflow. Use hosted payment pages/tokenization; **never** store card number or CVV. Verify webhook signatures and independently reconcile merchant payments, refunds, chargebacks, fees and COD remittances. No 'payment succeeded' based on browser return URL alone.
- Provider abstraction for delivery: compare and, after approval, integrate suitable carriers such as Cathedis, Sendit, Express Relais as contracts/API access permit. Support quote/selection where documented, shipment creation, label, pickup scheduling when supported, immutable tracking events, webhook polling fallback, failed delivery, returns, COD ledger and reconciliation. Provider failures must not block company warehouse operations and must not duplicate labels/charges.
- Admin can choose fulfilment from a warehouse, pickup vs delivery if operationally supported, packaging weight/dimensions, shipping fees and service regions; distinguish stock on hand from salable, reserved and fulfilled.
- Price version and discount snapshot frozen on order, currency MAD exact decimals, tax treatment and invoice/legal texts reviewed by a Moroccan accountant/legal professional before public sale.
